Privacy Policy for Precision Classic Last updated: September 26, 2026 Precision Classic is a Wear OS watch face developed by Prabhat Pandey. This Privacy Policy explains how Precision Classic handles user information. Data Collection Precision Classic does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The watch face does not require users to create an account and does not operate its own backend server for collecting user data. Watch Data Precision Classic may display information provided by the Wear OS platform or watch complications, such as: Heart rate Step count Battery level Date and day Other supported watch information This information is used only to display the relevant information on the watch face. Precision Classic does not upload this information to any external server controlled by the developer. Permissions The watch face may rely on Wear OS system features or complication data providers to display information selected by the user. Any access to watch or health-related information is handled through Android and Wear OS APIs and is subject to the permissions and privacy controls provided by the operating system and the respective data provider. Third-Party Services Precision Classic does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or other third-party services for collecting personal information. Data Sharing Precision Classic does not sell, trade, transfer, or share personal information with third parties. Children's Privacy Precision Classic does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Precision Classic changes. Any updated version of this policy will be published on the same publicly accessible privacy policy page with an updated revision date.
