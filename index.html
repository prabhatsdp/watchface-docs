Privacy Policy

Oceanic Minimal
Effective date: 20 September 2026

Oceanic Minimal is a Wear OS watch face developed and published by Prabhat Pandey. This Privacy Policy explains how the watch face handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

Oceanic Minimal does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information or sensitive user data. The watch face does not require you to create an account or provide personal details.

Device and Watch Information

The watch face may use information provided by Wear OS that is necessary to display the watch face, such as the current time and date. This information is processed on your device and is not collected, stored, or transmitted to the developer.

Permissions

Oceanic Minimal does not request access to location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, fitness data, or other sensitive device information.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Oceanic Minimal does not use analytics services, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or third-party services that collect user data.

Data Sharing and Retention

Because Oceanic Minimal does not collect personal data, the developer does not share personal data with third parties and does not retain personal data.

Children's Privacy

Oceanic Minimal does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Oceanic Minimal changes or if legal or platform requirements change. Any updated version will be published at the same public privacy policy URL with a revised effective date.
