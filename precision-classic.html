Privacy Policy

Precision Classic
Effective date: 26 September 2026

Precision Classic is a Wear OS watch face developed and published by Prabhat Pandey. This Privacy Policy explains how the watch face handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

Precision Classic does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information or sensitive user data. The watch face does not require you to create an account or provide personal details.

Device and Watch Information

Precision Classic may display information provided by Wear OS or supported complication data providers, including the current time, date, day, battery level, step count, heart rate, and other information selected by the user.

This information is used only to display relevant information on the watch face. Precision Classic does not transmit this information to the developer or to a server controlled by the developer.

Health and Fitness Information

Precision Classic may display health or fitness information, such as heart rate and step count, when this information is made available by Wear OS or a complication data provider.

The developer does not independently collect, store, transmit, or retain this health or fitness information. Access to such information is controlled by Wear OS, the relevant data provider, and the permissions configured on your device.

Permissions

Precision Classic relies on Wear OS system features and complication data providers to display supported information. Any permissions required for specific information are managed through Wear OS and the respective data provider.

Precision Classic does not use permissions to collect personal information for the developer.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Precision Classic does not use advertising SDKs, analytics services, tracking technologies, or third-party services for collecting or profiling user information.

Data Sharing and Retention

Because Precision Classic does not collect personal data, the developer does not sell, share, or retain personal data.

Children's Privacy

Precision Classic does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Precision Classic changes or if legal or platform requirements change. Any updated version will be published at the same public privacy policy URL with a revised effective date.
